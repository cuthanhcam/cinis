# Code of conduct

## Expected behavior

Treat people with respect, welcome different experience levels, and make technical disagreements specific and constructive. Explain evidence, uncertainty, and trade-offs. Critique code and ideas without attacking their author. Give credit for contributions and respect privacy.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, stalking, sexualized conduct, repeated personal insults, deliberate disruption, and publication of private information are unacceptable. Do not retaliate against a person who reports a concern or participates in its resolution.

## Scope

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and project-associated spaces, including conduct while representing the project. Technical criticism and good-faith questions are welcome; repeated hostile behavior is not.

## Reporting and response

Report concerns privately through an established maintainer channel. No dedicated reporting email is configured; do not disclose sensitive report details in public issues. The maintainer reviews available evidence discreetly and may request clarification. A personal project cannot promise a response-time SLA or absolute confidentiality.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit/remove harmful content, request a behavior change, restrict interaction, or temporarily/permanently exclude a participant according to severity and repetition. Decisions should focus on observed conduct and protecting participation. A participant may provide additional context privately for reconsideration.

Security vulnerabilities follow SECURITY.md. Usage and design questions follow .github/SUPPORT.md.
